# Second Sight: The Assistant Learns to Look

The assistant can now look at a photograph and discuss it, and listen when you would rather speak than type. Around that, the writing surface gains bold and italics, a doodle canvas and saved views; the palette gains thirty-two themes in eight families of background; and every confirmation gains an editor. All of it lands on Android and Windows on the same day, because there is still only one heart.

**The assistant learned to look.** Import an mmproj file beside your local model and the assistant — still running entirely on your own hardware, still telling the internet precisely nothing — will examine a photograph and discuss it like a mildly clairvoyant librarian. A microphone now waits in notes, tasks and chat: on Android it needs no setup whatsoever, while Windows quietly borrows the transcription service you have already configured. A rapid model-switcher sits on both platforms, and for small models and free tiers there is Caveman Mode, which sets aside the clever internal prompting and simply lets the model speak.

**Writing grew richer.** Bold, italics and their friends, with exports that keep the formatting intact. A doodle canvas for the thoughts words cannot reach. Drafts sit politely beside the recycle bin, waiting rather than gone. A note can hide behind its own individual lock. A search you keep typing can be kept as a saved view of its own, so "overdue and important" becomes a place you visit rather than a phrase you retype, exactly as promised. Every one of these leaves your work where you left it, with the new surface laid over the old rather than in place of it.

**The look of the place grew up.** Thirty-two themes, backgrounds in eight style families, and a Random companion that ambles through them on its own schedule. The drifting blobs keep a wall-clock heartbeat on their own thread rather than borrowing the screen's frame clock, so whenever the interface is busy they are exactly where they should be the moment the frames resume. Glass, gradient and colour arrive together, and none of them asks for attention. A background that keeps its own time is a background you never catch out of position, whatever else the machine is doing at the time.

**Housekeeping improved, and the confirmations came of age.** Share any note, task or conversation as a tidy file, or let automatic backups tick away on a schedule; the same encrypted, portable format crosses devices and platforms alike. Every mutating proposal arrives in a dialog that is itself the editor, with every arguable field filled and editable before anything is written, so "actually, no" costs nothing and leaves nothing behind. The dialog is the last place a change can be reconsidered, and this release makes it a comfortable place to say no. Sharing is a tap, and the file that results is tidy enough to send on without a second thought.

Nothing reaches the database until you say so, and nothing leaves portably without being previewed first. Drafts, individual locks, saved searches and exports all meet that standard. The same four languages welcome each arrival by name, and the first sight is still there behind the new one, doing its quiet work.
