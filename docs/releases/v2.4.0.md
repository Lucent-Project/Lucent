# Thirty-Two Moods and a Cat Who Means It

The background has been given a life of its own, the launch cat has been told to hurry, and the look of the app has grown from sixteen themes to thirty-two. Each is a real, distinct colour with a real, distinct name, and eight style families of backgrounds sit beneath them. It is a polish release in the best sense: the same app you use, with every edge rubbed smooth and a floor of new moods under it.

**The launch gets on with it.** The opening animation runs 1.3× faster — the splash speed constant moved from 0.8 to 0.615 — and the cat still waves, blinks and becomes glass, with considerably less ceremony. The shapes it draws on the way, the waving paw and the blink and the turn to glass, are untouched; what has changed is only how long the visit takes. The code keeps the entire slimming history in a comment beside that constant, so the number, and the reasoning behind it, travel together, and the next piece of tuning starts from a known place.

**The background keeps its own time.** The drifting blobs advance from wall-clock time on their own Default-dispatcher coroutine, so their position is a function of the clock and of nothing else the interface may be doing; the auto-cycle's phase advances the same way, on its own dispatcher. Eight style families — Dawn, Blossom, Evergreen, Aqua, Sunfire, Vivid, Earth and Velvet — carry the 42 boards and their 126 unique colours, fifteen or eighteen apiece. Beside Cycle sits Random, which changes palette every twenty seconds and declines to repeat itself. Random has its own settings row and a short hint, and it is wired on both platforms.

**Thirty-two themes, all of them real.** System, Light and Dark sit beside thirty-two colour themes, drawn eight light-and-dark pairs at a time: Honey and Umber, Mint and Deep Moss, Periwinkle and Blueberry, Coral and Brick, Snowdrop and Obsidian, Honeydew and Fir, Linen and Walnut, Frost and Abyss. Every backdrop colour is unique across the whole of the set, every name is unique, and all four interface languages know each of them by name, with their own labels and descriptions. The appearance settings render their sections straight from the family list, so those eight groups are the single source of that arrangement.

**The look is chosen where you can see it.** Settings previews each theme with its swatch, its name in your language and its actual backdrop colour, so nobody picks an adjective and hopes for a paint. The lock screen runs a live countdown that ticks once per second, and a wrong-password hint clears itself three seconds after the last attempt, so a stale banner has nowhere to linger. The launch cat, the blobs, the themes and the glass are the same on both platforms, because the two machines share one heart, which is the arrangement the release is built on.

The composer and the list surfaces were tidied in the same breath, template chips carry proper icons, and the drift clock and the auto-cycle keep their own heartbeat. Thirty-two themes, eight background families, four languages, two platforms, one heartbeat, and a cat that waves faster. The floor beneath it is broad, and every shade on it has a name.
