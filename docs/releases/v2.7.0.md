# A Place for Everything

Every version of Lucent has been about putting the right thing where you can find it. This one is about putting it where you can see it. The pages where notes and tasks are born now open on one calm level, with everything that configures an item a single tap away, and the settings that shape the app follow the same principle. What you are writing stays in front of you; what you are deciding waits politely behind a fold.

**A place for everything.** The note and task composers show only what you are writing: title, details, attachments, Save, and one-tap templates on a brand-new note. Everything that configures the item lives one level down behind a labelled "More options" fold — mode, colour, tags, pin, priority, reminder and repeat — built once in the shared module as the MoreOptionsFold and used by both platforms. The fold starts collapsed on every fresh composer and remembers its state while you work, so a long editing session is never interrupted by a stray tap, and the editorial surface stays exactly as wide as the page.

**Colours from your wallpaper.** Settings → Appearance leads with Material You dynamic colour: on Android 12 and later the wallpaper palette takes the highest priority in the app, and the theme, backdrop, ink and the drifting blobs recolour themselves from its primary, secondary and tertiary tones. Stored choices are never destroyed; dynamic colour overrides the read path, and switching it off restores your theme and palette byte for byte, on the same frame. Banners on the Theme and Background pages explain the arrangement clearly, and the desktop keeps its own palette and ignores the flag, a distinction it states plainly.

**Pickers that answer, and dates that change when you do.** The assistant's Photos entry opens the system photo picker, the most dependable route a third-party app has to your albums, with the documents picker one tap away where a vendor gallery is particularly opinionated. Cloud storage is offered when a real cloud provider is installed, and explained when none is. Switching language changes the dates under every note and task card on the same frame, through a shared language-keyed formatting helper, and both trash suffixes speak all four languages in the same pass as the catalogue they belong to, so every language keeps up at once.

**The locks count, and the backups keep up.** Every password prompt in the app — lock screen, disabling the lock, the danger zone, the hidden area, backup restore — charges one shared counter, with attempts-left hints, live mm:ss countdowns that survive a restart, and an optional self-destruct. A successful unlock resets it, and one definition of what hidden means now serves every vault gate. Backup manifest v12 carries every user-visible preference, saved searches included, and auto-backup arms itself at startup on Android and on Windows. Every appearance choice stays byte-identical across a Material You session, whichever way the switch is thrown.

What remains is what you feel: a new note is one calm page, the fold holds its contents until asked, a language change moves the dates at once, the assistant's Photos button opens your gallery, every lock keeps the same time, and a backup finds even the preferences you had forgotten you set.
