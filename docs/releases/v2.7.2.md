# The Promises Get Their Follow-Through

Promises are only worth the arrangements behind them. The two-level composers are wired and reachable on both platforms; the lock that counts wrong passwords declines the shortcut for as long as it counts; the drifting background keeps time with the screen's own frame callback; and templates become something you write. Nothing here is a new idea; everything here is an arrangement that answers when you press it.

**A lock that means it.** A wrong-password count is a promise about time, and the lock screens read it reactively. The moment a round of wrong guesses closes, the fingerprint button on Android and Windows Hello on the desktop step out of the room, and a live countdown takes their place — one that survives recomposition, closing the app and restarting it, because PasswordAttempts is the single persisted source of truth for every password gate in the app. A biometric prompt already open when a cooldown begins is re-checked against the counter before it unlocks. Locked out is an unambiguous state on both platforms.

**A background that keeps time with your screen.** The drift clock now advances on the interface thread's own frame callback, quantised to whole vsyncs: two at 60 Hz, three at 90, four at 120. Positions are pure functions of elapsed time, so redraws arrive evenly spaced with no scheduler jitter, no background-thread snapshot writes, and nothing queued while the interface is busy. The Random option crossfades into the next palette over about 1.8 seconds, from a seeded pick that never repeats the one before. The motion is the motion it always was; it simply arrives on time.

**A composer with two levels, and templates you write yourself.** Pin-to-top now sits on the main level directly under the title, while colour, tags, mode switches, priority, reminder and repeat wait behind the fold; switch a mode on and its editor appears on the main level. A "New template" chip after the four built-ins turns the blank composer into a template editor, where the title is the name. Saved templates join the strip, a long press deletes one, and leaving mid-authoring parks a draft that returns as "Continue unfinished template". The assistant header carries its "+" for a brand-new conversation, and long titles ellipsize cleanly.

**Colours, fewer and accountable.** The theme picker offers exactly 18 tints, nine light and nine dark; the background picker offers 36 boards. Near-duplicates have been pruned, every name and hex verified unique, and stored keys for retired choices still resolve, so an existing selection is never displaced. With Material You active on Android the two colour rows are hidden and the banners stay; the flag holds the top of the appearance page while it is active, and the rows return when it is not. The "(auto)" captions have left all four languages, and ten new template strings have joined them. A catalogue, then, not a crowd.

Backups move to v13, carrying the Material You flag, multi-attachment chats, custom templates and the authoring draft; the release pipeline sits in the repository, byte-identical and inspectable; and no database migration was needed, because templates and drafts are settings JSON. The lock means it, the background keeps time, the colours are honest, and your own templates are waiting after the built-ins.
