# Fewer Words, More Colour

The settings pages now lead with the switch, and everything else has been given back to you as silence: forty-seven catalogue entries retired from all four languages. What remains is colour with more ambition, three weights walking their own cycles, and a section model derived from a single list, so a card always belongs to exactly one place. The dialogs are untouched, word for word, and your data is exactly where you left it.

**Shorter pages, and every word that earns its keep.** The explanatory paragraph that sat under a switch — what Markdown does, what rich text costs, what a memory tier means, why a history cap exists — has retired, and three over-long navigation subtitles went with it. Titles, switches, values, empty states and every notice explaining why something is unavailable have stayed. All 47 catalogue entries left all four languages, so the pages are shorter in English, Chinese, Japanese and Korean alike. Every confirmation, consent notice and warning remains exactly where it was, word for word, because that is where an explanation earns its keep.

**A gradient with three colours and its own opinions.** Each of the three colour weights walks slowly through the palette on its own cycle — 37, 49 and 61 seconds per step — crossfading the whole time and starting a third of the palette apart, while the waves beneath them run on shorter periods of 23, 29 and 37 seconds. Measured change is 8/255 per channel after three seconds and 22/255 after eight, and yet no two neighbouring pixels differ by more than 5/255, so the field reads as softness rather than edges. Reduced motion freezes the waves and leaves the colours walking.

**One list, one section, and no drift between them.** The section model is derived from the same list the page renders, so the two cannot disagree. A test pins both the order of the sections and the rule that an item appears in exactly one of them, so a card always belongs to exactly one place and stays there. That single source of truth makes the whole class of disagreement impossible rather than merely absent.

**Writes that record what happened and carry on.** Background writes record their outcome in the on-device event log and keep running, so every write has an outcome on the record and the stream continues regardless. The four writes you actually wait on — saving a note, saving a task, and either pin toggle — report in your own language. This is the same courtesy Crash Shield has promised the interface thread for a while, now kept by the background as well: an outcome exists for every write, whether or not you were waiting on it. Android and Windows still compile one shared source tree.

Nothing about your data changed: no new settings and no schema change, with the .lcb backup and the encrypted store exactly as they were. The pages are quieter, the colour is louder, and every card knows where it lives.
