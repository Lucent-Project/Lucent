# The Assistant Shows Its Working

Ask the assistant something and it now shows its working: a live, collapsible trace of the reasoning it chooses to show you, one line per tool it calls, and whether that call finished, failed or was cancelled. Around it, search has moved into the box where you type, every list renders what is really stored on the first frame, and one pinning control does the one thing it says. Nothing here needs to be learned; the app simply behaves like itself.

**An assistant that shows its working.** The trace is driven by real events from the model stream rather than a decorative animation, and it is kept with the reply, so reopening an old conversation finds the working still there and backups carry it. A tool call is one line with an outcome: finished, failed or cancelled. The assistant gains nine notebook tools, so notes and tasks can be filed and named in the same conversation. The model switcher on the chat page lists the models you already chose, fetched once and selected together.

**Every first frame tells the truth.** Fifty-odd settings now come from a cache seeded at startup, so every switch, list, sub-page and Material You card renders what is really stored on the very first frame — the stored value, first time and thereafter. Every list — archived, draft, hidden, trash, completed and the notebook sub-pages — remembers where you were scrolled to. A note opened from the Privacy area closes back into the Privacy area, and the back arrow and the system gesture do the same thing.

**One pin that works, and writes that mean what they say.** There is a single pinning control, and it works. Saving, pinning and reordering write exactly what they intend to and nothing else, so an edit made in one place stays made. Clearing everything clears the notebooks too, and reports its outcome either way.

**Colour and cards with some life in them.** Three broad hue bands run along one diagonal with a slow wash along the other, so several colours share the whole screen and fade out smoothly, full-window and edge to edge; your Material You wallpaper colours ride the gradient rather than only the flat theme. Dragging wobbles every card gently like jelly while your finger is down, and dragging one card over another previews the swap live: nothing moves for real until you let go, and letting go of nothing springs everything back.

The editor grows with what you type, keeps consecutive blank lines, and expands to a real full screen, with Markdown and rich text able to coexist on one note. Setup offers six API choices with their addresses built in, twenty profiles, and a model picker with square tick boxes that remembers the ones you ticked. What the release leaves you with is an app whose first frame is already right, a trace kept with the reply, and a pin that does exactly what it says.
