# The Swipe Becomes Natural

You know that moment when you're flipping between Notes and Tasks and you think "I wish I could just... swipe"? Well, now you can. The bottom bar still works — tap away, nobody's taking that from you — but a horizontal swipe now carries you between tabs with a fluidity that makes you wonder why it wasn't always there. The pager keeps your place, remembers which screen you were on, and doesn't rebuild everything from scratch when you come back. It's the sort of feature that, once you've used it once, feels like it must have been there all along.

While we were making things move, the text editor got a quieter but equally important upgrade: the field now tracks where your cursor is and scrolls to keep it visible above the keyboard. No more typing blind, no more hunting for your place after the keyboard pops up. The scroll follows the cursor through selection changes, deletions, and the general mayhem of editing, so what you're looking at is always what you're typing into.

The settings pages also received a small exorcism of their own. A handful of them — Language, Theme, Background, Memory — were using an initial value that could briefly be `null` before the real setting arrived, which meant the page would flash a half-rendered state before settling down. Now they wait until the value is actually there before showing anything. No flash, no half-drawn page, just the setting you expected, right when you expect it.

Version 2.8.3 also brought the release workflow into the world, so every future release gets its APK and EXE built and uploaded automatically. The README was rewritten in its own voice, because a product description should sound like the product. Your encrypted data is still encrypted, your backups still back up, and your assistant still assists — nothing changed about the things that protect you, only about the things you touch every day.

**Swipe between tabs** — HorizontalPager with state sync, zero rebuilds.
**Cursor follows your eyes** — text field auto-scroll keeps your place above the keyboard.
**Settings that stay put** — no more flash of nothing before the real value appears.
**Automated builds** — every release gets its APK and EXE without manual labour.
**Same encryption, same backup, same assistant, same everything that matters.**
