# The Pen Writes, the Watchman Waits, and Every Page Remembers

Lucent 2.9.1 is a release of small mercies: the page you were on, still there when you return; a switch that shows your setting instead of its own opinion; a pen that writes the name and then lets the ink go.

**Appearance learns calligraphy.** Settings → Appearance carries a Splash animation page. A switch sits at the top, two choices below. The cat waves, blinks and turns to glass, and remains the default. The pen is new: it enters from the left, draws Lucent across the centre in script, and lets the ink fade until only your notes are left. Turn the switch off and Lucent goes straight to your work over the theme and its moving background.

**Settings open on your values.** The automatic-backup switch and the cloud fields come from the same cache the rest of Settings uses, so the page opens on what you chose. Cloud storage keeps its own counsel too: with the switch off, the rows beneath stay out of the way.

**A tab lands where you left it.** Tapping a tab enters that page in the same frame, on the page you were last at. Leave a half-written note behind and it is still there on return; the quiet save prompt stays for the moment you step out of the app itself.

**The API page keeps your edits until you ask.** Fetching and choosing models fills the draft only; everything is written together when you tap Save API, and a small line says so until then.

**The composer keeps pace with the keyboard.** The writing field lifts clear of the keyboard as the caret moves, so the line you are editing stays in view. The save button has shed its plus sign and centred its label; the draft button sits square in its capsule.

**Shizuku has a watchman.** While the advanced switch is on, Lucent asks after Shizuku once a minute. Should the answer go missing, the switch turns off and a prompt, shaped to what was found, explains what to do: a missing Shizuku offers its official GitHub releases to the browser; a stopped one offers to open; a running one asks for permission, confirmed in Shizuku's own dialog. Once the switch is off, the watchman stands down until called back.

**Updates tidy up after themselves.** An update downloads quietly, installs when you say so, and removes its installer afterwards. If the file has not arrived in twenty minutes, the fragment goes and Lucent says so. Choose Later and it asks again next launch. The small line under Check for updates fades after three seconds.

**The assistant keeps hold of what it just made.** Whatever it creates or edits stays on a short list carried with the conversation, so "add a line to it" finds the right note, and a title that misses reports the closest ones that do exist.

**Small housekeeping, done properly.** Clearing the logs also stops the recording. Optimise for small models has moved to Personalization. Settings remembers where you were, page by page. The font list drops a line, and the import button takes half the room.

Your notes stay encrypted on the device, an .lcb backup carries every one of these choices along with them, and the log keeps its record of each step.
