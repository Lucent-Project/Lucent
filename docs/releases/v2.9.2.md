# Tasks and Notes Move In Together, and the Library Gets a Drawer

Lucent 2.9.2 rearranges the furniture. Nothing has been thrown out; everything has simply been given a room of its own, and the rooms now connect in the order a sensible person would walk through them.

**Home is where both lists live.** The first tab on Android is now Home, and it holds your tasks and your notes side by side. Two labels sit at the top of the page, each with a small dot beside it; the filled dot marks where you are. Tap the other and the page slides across in a quarter of a second, with each list exactly as you left it: the half-written note, the scroll position, the open detail. Home remembers which of the two you used last and opens there next time.

**The drawer holds the library.** Three lines in the top-left corner open a frosted panel from the left edge. Inside, in this order: Notebooks, Drafts, Archive, Trash, and Hidden when you have opened it. Each row carries a quiet count, and the list follows the mode you are in, so Archive means archived notes under Notes and completed tasks under Tasks. The same two dots sit at the top of the drawer, should you prefer to change lists without closing it. The back button closes the drawer before it does anything else.

**Notebooks have a tab of their own.** The second tab is Notebooks, with every notebook you have made and a running total at the top. Open a note or task from a notebook and closing it brings you straight back to the notebook, with its contents still unfolded. From Notebooks or the Assistant, the back button takes you home rather than out of the app.

**One search, sensibly ordered.** The search field on Home now travels with you: type a query under Tasks, switch to Notes, and the words come along. Results from the list you are looking at come first; below a clear rule, everything else Lucent knows about follows, so nothing is ever more than a scroll away.

**Windows gets a longer sidebar.** On the desktop the sidebar now reads Tasks and Notes, then Notebooks, Drafts, Archive and Trash, then the Assistant, Search, Insights and Settings, in three tidy groups. Drafts, Archive and Trash each carry the same pair of dots, so one page serves both notes and tasks. Opening an archived note and closing it returns you to Archive, and a draft opens directly in the editor.

**The checks are more thorough.** Every change now passes through a longer inspection before it reaches you. The shared logic is tested on Android and on the desktop from a single set of tests, the desktop suite also runs on Windows itself, encryption is proven end to end on both Linux and Windows, and a hygiene pass confirms balanced brackets and sensibly sized files. Each run finishes with a tidy table of what was tested.

Your notes stay encrypted on the device, an .lcb backup carries every setting along with them, including the page you last had open, and the log records each step through the new rooms: every mode switch, every drawer visit, every page opened.
