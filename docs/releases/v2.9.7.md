# The Assistant Borrows a Proper Toolkit, and Finally Tidies Up After Itself

Lucent 2.9.7 is a patch with three sorts of tidying in it: the assistant's reasoning dials, the assistant's styling kit, and the small pile of installers that used to live in your storage forever.

**Reasoning, in each vendor's own terms.** Every provider publishes its own list of thinking depths, and they disagree with each other cheerfully. Lucent now reads those lists properly. OpenAI gets none, minimal, low, medium, high, xhigh or max where the model genuinely accepts them; Claude gets adaptive thinking with low through max; Gemini 3 uses thinking levels while the 2.5 family keeps its token budget; Kimi and DeepSeek get low, high and max, with an off switch where one is offered. If a model's maker publishes no such dial, the picker simply does not appear rather than sitting there explaining itself, and switching models shows exactly what the new one accepts.

**Cheaper conversations.** Every provider offers prompt caching, and Lucent now goes out of its way to earn it. The parts of a request that never change — instructions, tool definitions, the earlier conversation — go first and byte for byte identical, while everything that moves (the clock, what you touched recently, notes from your other chats) travels at the end where it cannot spoil the prefix. Claude requests carry explicit cache breakpoints on the tools, the instructions and the last message; OpenAI and Kimi requests carry a stable cache key and their documented cache settings. When a provider reports how much of the prompt came from its cache, the assistant's step list says so, and the log keeps the tally.

**Longer memories for thinking models.** DeepSeek, Kimi and Claude want the model's own reasoning handed back to it on later turns when tools are in play, and they are firm about it. Those turns are now kept and replayed, so a long tool-using conversation holds its thread instead of starting again from cold.

**A richer typing kit.** The assistant could already make words bold or italic; it can now also set five text sizes, choose from nine text colours and eight highlighter colours, and apply any of it to a chosen phrase or to an entire note. When it needs to know what is already styled, read_note_formatting tells it, in words, before it touches anything. The same sizes and colours sit in the editor's own toolbar, so what the assistant sets is what you can edit by hand afterwards.

**Notebooks, explained.** read_notebook now describes one in full — cover, pinning, dates, what it holds, and which other notebooks those same notes and tasks also appear in — while update_notebook renames, recolours and pins in a single step. Asking the assistant what is inside a notebook now gets you an actual answer.

**Housekeeping.** A downloaded installer used to linger in your storage like a guest who will not take the hint. Lucent now checks on startup whether the update actually went in, and if it did the package is removed — from its working folder and from the folder it was copied to — on both Android and Windows. The same check runs the moment a silent install finishes.

Everything from before works as it did: notes, tasks, notebooks, encrypted backups, and an assistant that answers in your language.
